About this arcade

PC Commander Arcade is a fan-made tribute to the PC Commander Alexa skill — the one that lets you say things like “Alexa, tell my computer to play music” and watches your PC obey.

This arcade has no real Pushbullet integration, no accounts, no servers, and no PII. Just three little games sharing one neon synthwave vibe:

  • Push Defender — a shoot-em-up where you triage the firehose of push notifications.
  • Voice Commander — Simon Says with Alexa's voice, in six languages.
  • Echo Synth — a constellation maker that turns your phrases into glowing star maps.

Built in one night with HTML, CSS and a sprinkle of Web Audio. No build tools, no frameworks, no trackers.
